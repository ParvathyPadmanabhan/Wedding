Our Story

We met 10 years ago in college — not as best friends, not even close friends… just two people who knew each other.

For 7 years, life kept us in the same place but on different paths. Then life did something unexpected — it took us miles apart.

And somewhere in that distance, we realized what we truly meant to each other.

For the past 4 years, distance has been constant… but so has love — growing stronger with every mile.

From LONG DISTANCE to LONG TERM ❤️
