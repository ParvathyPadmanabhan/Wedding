Our Story

FROM LONG DISTANCE TO LONG TERM ❤️

Some stories are written
slowly… and beautifully.

We met 10 years ago in college — not as best friends, not even close friends… just two people who knew each other.

For 7 years, life kept us in the same place yet somehow on completely different paths. And then, life did something unexpected — it took us miles apart.

Somewhere within that distance, between late-night conversations, missed moments, airports and endless calls, we realized what we truly meant to each other.

For the past 4 years, distance has been constant… but so has love — growing stronger with every mile, every wait, and every reunion.
